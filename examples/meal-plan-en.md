# One-Week Dinner Plan

Monday, December 14 to Sunday, December 20, 2026. For two people, heavy on vegetables and making use of batch cooking.

## Meal Plan

| Day | Main dish | Side dish | Soup |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Teriyaki chicken | Spinach with dashi dressing | Miso soup with wakame and tofu |
| Tue | Nikujaga (meat and potato stew) | Cucumber in sweet vinegar | Daikon miso soup |
| Wed | Salmon meuniere | Broccoli salad | Corn soup |
| Thu | Mapo tofu | Glass noodle salad | Egg drop soup |
| Fri | Simmered yellowtail and daikon | Simmered hijiki | Clam miso soup |
| Sat | Hamburger steak | Potato salad | Onion soup |
| Sun | Hot pot (yosenabe) | Lightly pickled napa cabbage | (served from the pot) |

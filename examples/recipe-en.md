# Nikujaga (Japanese Meat and Potato Stew)

A classic home-style dish: fluffy potatoes soaked in a sweet and savory broth. It cooks in no time, and it tastes even better if you let it rest overnight so the flavors can settle in.

## At a Glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Servings | 4 |
| Prep time | 15 min |
| Cook time | 25 min |
| Difficulty | Easy |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Thinly sliced beef | 200 g |
| Potatoes | 3 (about 450 g) |
| Onion | 1 |
| Carrot | 1 |
| Shirataki noodles | 1 pack (150 g) |
| Green beans | 6 |
| Vegetable oil | 1 tbsp |
| Dashi stock | 300 ml |
| Sugar | 2 tbsp |
| Soy sauce | 3 tbsp |
| Mirin | 2 tbsp |
| Sake | 2 tbsp |

## Directions

1. Peel the potatoes, cut them into bite-size pieces, and soak in water for 5 minutes. Cut the onion into wedges and the carrot into rough chunks.
2. Cut the shirataki into easy-to-eat lengths, blanch briefly, and drain. Trim the green beans and cut them in half.
3. Heat the oil in a pot and stir-fry the beef until it changes color.
4. Add the onion, carrot, potatoes, and shirataki, and stir-fry for about 2 minutes.
5. Add the dashi, sugar, sake, and mirin, bring to a boil, and skim off the foam.
6. Cover with a drop lid and simmer over medium-low heat for 10 minutes.
7. Add the soy sauce and simmer for another 10 minutes. Add the green beans and cook for about 2 minutes, then serve.

## Tips

> **Tip:** Add the sugar first and the soy sauce later so the flavors soak in better.
>
> To keep the potatoes from breaking apart, cut them larger and avoid stirring too much.
